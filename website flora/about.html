Who We Are

LONG – International Family Movement

"Welcome to LONG – International Family Movement. We are an international humanitarian organization committed to protecting children, strengthening families, empowering youth, and building stronger communities through education, humanitarian action, leadership development, and sustainable social initiatives."

Our Mission

To protect vulnerable children, strengthen families, empower youth, and promote sustainable community development through humanitarian assistance, education, leadership training, psychological support, and social innovation.

Our Vision

To build a world where every child is protected, every family is strengthened, and every community has access to opportunities that promote dignity, resilience, education, and lasting development.

Together, we believe every child deserves hope, dignity, protection, and the opportunity to build a better future.

Deeply Rooted in Purpose

LONG – International Family Movement is an international humanitarian and community development organization dedicated to improving the lives of vulnerable children, strengthening families, empowering youth, and promoting sustainable development across communities.

Founded on the principles of compassion, integrity, service, and leadership, the organization works through humanitarian projects, educational initiatives, community outreach, youth empowerment programs, and international partnerships.

Our goal is to build stronger communities by investing in people, especially children and young leaders, while promoting peace, dignity, and hope.
